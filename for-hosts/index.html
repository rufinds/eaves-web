What you can do

Create your event

Add everything people need to know: event details, dates, location, price, ticket link, age limit, genres and images. Save a draft and publish when you’re ready.

Build your lineup

Put together your lineup stage by stage, with artists and set times. Add registered artists or guest artists, send invitations and get notified about their responses. Eaves also flags overlapping slots.

Connect with venues

Find venues on Eaves and request to host your event there. The venue owner can review and approve or decline your request in the app.

Manage and share

Edit, duplicate or cancel your events and keep an eye on Going and Interested responses. Every public event also gets its own shareable page on eaves.be.

Questions

How do I become a host?

Choose Host during onboarding or add the role later in the app. The role is available right away, so you can start creating public events immediately.

Can I also create private events?

Yes. Everyone can create private events and invite friends, for example for pre-drinks or a house party. The Host role gives you the tools to create public events.

Can I add artists who aren’t on Eaves?

Yes. You can add guest artists without an Eaves account, as well as registered artists and curated profiles. Eaves also flags overlapping time slots.

How do venue requests work?

Choose a venue on Eaves and send a request for your event. The venue owner can approve or decline it in the app. You’ll be notified of their decision.

Do you sell tickets?

No. You can add an external ticket link to your event. Ticket sales are handled by the platform you link to.

How do people discover my events?

Public events can appear in people’s feeds and search results, including saved searches that match the event. Each public event also gets its own shareable page on eaves.be.

Does it cost anything?

No. Eaves is completely free for every role: users, hosts, artists and venues.

Can I have multiple roles?

Yes. One account can have multiple roles, such as Artist and Host. You can switch between active roles in your profile.
