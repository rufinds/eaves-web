What you can do

Open to every artist

Just getting started or already playing shows? Every artist is welcome on Eaves. Choose the Artist role and create your profile for free. No following or approval needed.

Your music, your profile

Show people who you are. Add your artist name, bio, genres, social links and booking contact. Feature tracks from Spotify, SoundCloud or YouTube so people can discover your sound.

Join event lineups

Hosts can invite you to perform at their events, with a specific stage and time slot. Accept or decline invitations in the app and keep track of your upcoming performances.

Build your following

Let people discover and follow you. Your profile brings together your music, upcoming and past performances, followers and ratings.

How to start

  1. Download EavesGet the app on iPhone or Android and create your account.
  2. Choose ArtistSelect Artist during onboarding or add the role later. You get access right away.
  3. Make it yoursYour artist profile is created automatically. Add your bio, genres, music, social links and booking contact.

Questions

Can anyone create an artist profile?

Yes. Eaves is open to every artist, from first-time DJs and bedroom producers to established musicians and bands. You don’t need a following or approval to get started.

How do I get invited to a lineup?

Hosts can add you to an event lineup and send you an invitation with a stage and time slot. You can accept or decline it in the app.

What can I add to my artist profile?

Your artist name, bio, genres, social links, booking contact and featured tracks from Spotify, SoundCloud or YouTube.

Who can see my artist profile?

Artist profiles are public. Visitors can see your profile, upcoming and past performances, followers and ratings.

Does it cost anything?

No. Eaves is completely free for every role: users, hosts, artists and venues.

Can I have multiple roles?

Yes. One account can have multiple roles, such as Artist and Host. You can switch between active roles in your profile.
