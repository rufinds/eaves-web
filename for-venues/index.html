What you can do

Showcase your venue

Create a public venue profile with your location, capacity, opening hours, photos and socials. Give visitors a better idea of your space before they arrive.

Stay in control of requests

Hosts can request your venue for their events. Review each request and approve or decline it in the app. You stay in control of what gets organised at your venue.

Keep track of events

See upcoming and past events, as well as open requests for your venues. Your dashboard also gives you access to venue statistics.

Connect with the scene

Make your venue easier to discover. People can explore your location, follow your venue and find the events taking place there.

How to start

  1. Download EavesGet the app on iPhone or Android and create your account.
  2. Apply as a venue ownerChoose Venue Owner in the app and submit your application. Our team reviews venue owner requests before approval.
  3. Verify your venueProvide your company name, Belgian company number (KBO/BCE), business address and any supporting documents requested during verification.

Questions

How do I register my venue?

Download Eaves, create an account and apply for the Venue Owner role. Our team reviews applications before granting access.

What information do I need for verification?

You’ll need your company name, Belgian company number (KBO/BCE), business address and any supporting documents we request. These details are used to verify your venue.

How do host requests work?

Hosts can request your venue for an event. You can review each request and approve or decline it in the app. When you decline, you provide a reason so the host knows your decision.

What can I manage in the app?

Your dashboard gives you an overview of upcoming and past events, open requests and statistics for your venues. You can also manage your public venue profile.

Does it cost anything?

No. Eaves is free for venue owners, just like every other role on the platform.
