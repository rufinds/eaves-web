Got questions?

Is Eaves free?

Yes. Eaves is free for everyone: users, hosts, artists and venues.

Which city does Eaves cover?

Eaves is all about music events in Leuven, day or night: club nights, student parties, concerts, jazz nights, festivals and more. More cities may follow.

Is Eaves available on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Download Eaves from the App Store or Google Play. The app is available in English and Dutch.

Do I need an account?

You can browse upcoming events on this website without an account. In the app, you’ll need a free account to say Going or Interested, add friends, follow artists, venues and hosts, and get notifications.

Who can see which events I go to?

You decide. You can choose who sees your profile, friends list, following list, RSVPs and searches: everyone, friends only or no one.

Can I add my own events?

Yes. Choose Host in the app to create public events or private events for your friends. Artists can also create a profile and join lineups. Venue owner requests are reviewed by our team. Read more for hosts, artists and venues.
