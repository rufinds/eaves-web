Questions

Is Eaves available on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Download Eaves from the App Store or Google Play.

Which cities does Eaves cover?

Eaves is live in Leuven. More cities may follow.

How do I add my event, venue or artist profile?

Create an account in the app and request the Host, Artist or Venue Owner role. Venue owners are verified before approval.

Do I have to share my location?

No. Location access is optional and only used to show events and venues near you.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, in the app under Account, Privacy settings, Delete account, or by emailing help@eaves.be. See the delete account page.
